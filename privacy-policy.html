Pebble Spill privacy

Developer: Zi Wu · Effective date: 16 November 2026

This notice covers the Pebble Spill game, its support website and email support. These handle information in different ways.

Offline game

Pebble Spill is a single-player puzzle with 24 built-in stages. It works offline and does not require an account. The game does not upload gameplay data, progress or preferences. It has no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, tracking feature or developer-operated cloud-save service.

Local progress and preferences

The game stores your current stage and move history, completed stages and best move counts, and your language, water-sound and haptic preferences on your device. These are used to restore play and retain your choices.

You can change language, sounds and haptics in Settings. Undo and Restart affect the current attempt and keep completed-stage records. There is no in-app cloud sync or save export. Deleting the app can remove its local progress and settings; device backups and restoration depend on iOS and your backup settings.

Device access

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Water sounds and haptic feedback use native Apple APIs.

Support website

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing into GitHub. See GitHub's hosting documentation.

These support pages contain no developer-added analytics scripts, advertising tags, embedded trackers or contact form. Website hosting logs are separate from the game, which does not upload your local progress.

Email support

If you choose to email wuhoodev@gmail.com, your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send are delivered through Gmail. They are used to understand and reply to your request. The game does not automatically send a support email or attach your progress.

Share only what is needed to explain the issue. For questions or requests concerning a message you have sent, contact the same address.

Apple services

App Store downloads and iOS system services are handled by Apple under its own policies and settings. Apple's processing is separate from the game's local saves and from messages you choose to send to support.

Contact

Developer: Zi Wu
Privacy and support: wuhoodev@gmail.com

For gameplay help, read the first-use FAQ.

Updates

If these practices change, this notice will be updated with a revised effective date.

石头涨水隐私说明

开发者：Zi Wu · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

本说明涵盖石头涨水游戏、支持网站及邮件支持。三者处理信息的方式不同。

离线游戏

石头涨水是一款内置 24 关的单人解谜游戏，可离线游玩，无需账号。游戏不上传操作数据、进度或偏好，没有广告、第三方分析 SDK、追踪功能或开发者运营的云存档服务。

本地进度与偏好

游戏会在当前设备保存正在玩的关卡及操作记录、已完成关卡及最佳步数，以及语言、水声和触感偏好，用于恢复游戏并保留你的选择。

你可以在设置中更改语言、水声和触感。撤销和重开只影响当前尝试，不会清除已完成关卡的记录。应用没有云同步或存档导出功能。删除应用可能移除本地进度和设置；设备备份与恢复取决于 iOS 和你的备份设置。

设备权限

游戏不申请位置、通讯录、相册、相机或麦克风权限。水声和触感反馈使用 Apple 原生 API。

支持网站

支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问时，GitHub 会出于安全目的记录并保存访客的 IP 地址，无论访客是否登录 GitHub。详情见 GitHub 托管说明。

这些支持页面没有开发者添加的分析脚本、广告标签、内嵌追踪器或联系表单。网站托管日志与游戏本身不同；游戏不会上传你的本地进度。

邮件支持

如果你主动向 wuhoodev@gmail.com 发邮件，你的发件地址、邮件内容及自行附上的附件会通过 Gmail 送达，用于了解和回复你的请求。游戏不会自动发送支持邮件，也不会自动附上进度。

请只提供说明问题所需的信息。如果对已经发送的邮件有疑问或相关请求，可以通过同一邮箱联系。

Apple 服务

App Store 下载和 iOS 系统服务由 Apple 按其政策和设置处理。Apple 的处理与本游戏的本地存档、你主动发送的支持邮件相互独立。
