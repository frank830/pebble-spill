Start playing

Pebble Spill is a free, offline puzzle game with 24 stages. Move reusable pebbles between linked cups and match every marked waterline.

  1. Tap a numbered pebble below the cups, then tap cup A, B or C.
  2. Its number is the space it takes. A full cup pushes excess water through the pipe to the next cup.
  3. Select that pebble again to move it to another cup or the tray. Water already sent downstream stays there.
  4. Match all three dashed waterlines within the move limit. Keep water from overflowing cup C.

First-use FAQ

Where are the full rules?

Tap the information button at the top of the game. Use the close button in the upper-right corner of the panel to return to your current attempt.

What should I try in stage 1?

Tap pebble 2, then cup A. Select pebble 2 again, then tap cup C. All three targets match in two moves. The same pebble can be used more than once.

Why did the water not come back?

The pipes send water downstream. Removing a pebble lowers its old cup's waterline without returning water from another cup. Move order matters.

How do I return a pebble to the tray?

Select its numbered pebble button, then tap the tray under the cups. Selecting a pebble alone does not use a move; a valid move to a different location does.

What if I run out of moves or spill water?

Use the curved-back arrow to undo, or the circular arrow to restart the current stage. Previously completed stages remain completed. If water is still animating, wait for it to finish before the next tap.

How do I choose a stage or change the language?

Tap the stage control near the top to choose any of the 24 stages. Open Settings to select System, English or 简体中文, and to change water sounds and haptic feedback.

Where is my progress saved?

On this device. The game saves the current attempt, completed stages and preferences locally. It has no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove progress, and there is no developer-operated save recovery service. Device backups depend on iOS and your settings.

Why can I not hear water or feel a tap?

Check the Water sounds and Haptic feedback switches in Settings. Also check device volume, audio output and silent mode. Haptic feedback depends on the device and system settings.

开始游玩

石头涨水是一款有 24 关的免费离线解谜游戏。搬动可重复使用的石头，让相连的三只杯子同时对齐目标水位。

  1. 先点水杯下方带数字的石头按钮，再点 A、B 或 C 杯。
  2. 数字表示石头占的空间。水杯满了，多出的水会沿管道流到下一杯。
  3. 再次选中同一颗石头，把它移到别的杯子或托盘。已经流走的水不会倒流。
  4. 在步数内同时对齐三条虚线，别让水从 C 杯流出去。

首次使用常见问题

完整规则在哪里？

点击游戏顶部的信息按钮。看完后点面板右上角的关闭按钮，即可回到当前这一局。

第一关可以怎么做？

先选数字 2 的石头，点 A 杯。再选同一颗石头，点 C 杯。两步后，三杯水位就会同时对齐目标。同一颗石头可以重复使用。

为什么拿走石头，水没有回来？

水只会沿管道向下游流。拿走石头会降低原杯的水位，不会把下一杯的水吸回来。因此操作顺序会影响结果。

怎么把石头放回托盘？

先点对应数字的石头按钮，再点水杯下方的托盘。只选择石头不消耗步数；有效移动到不同位置才消耗一步。

步数用完或水流出去了怎么办？

点击向后弯的箭头撤销一步，或点环形箭头重开本关。已完成关卡的记录不会因此清除。如果水流动画还没结束，稍等片刻再进行下一次操作。

怎么选关或切换语言？

点顶部的关卡按钮，可以选择 24 关中的任意一关。在设置中选择“跟随系统”、English 或简体中文，也可开关水声和触感反馈。

进度保存在哪里？

保存在当前设备。游戏会在本地保存当前尝试、已完成关卡和偏好，没有账号或云同步。删除应用可能清除进度，也没有开发者运营的存档恢复服务。设备备份取决于 iOS 和你的设置。

为什么没有水声或触感？

先检查设置里的水声和触感开关，再检查设备音量、声音输出和静音模式。触感也取决于设备及系统设置。
